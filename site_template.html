How leagues compare

Scoring in the Highland League is easier than scoring in the Championship, so every player's goals and assists are converted onto a League Two scale before anyone is compared.

A figure below 1 means a weaker league, so that player's numbers are scaled down. Above 1 means a stronger league, scaled up. The figures come from players who actually moved between these leagues and how their scoring changed.

Leagues in other countries are rated the same way. Where only a few moves are on record, the figure is a cautious estimate (shaded) and seasons there count for less in a player's rating.

What this tool can and cannot do

It can

  • Look across far more players than anyone has time to watch.
  • Compare players from different leagues fairly.
  • Weigh several seasons, not one lucky run, and show who is improving or has stepped up a level.
  • Show contract end dates where Transfermarkt has them.

It cannot

  • Judge style, attitude, fitness or how a player fits the dressing room. That still needs someone to watch him.
  • Tell apart two strikers with the same tally but very different games. That needs paid match data such as Wyscout.
  • Guarantee a contract date is current. Check before approaching a player.
Statistics from FotMob. Contract dates from Transfermarkt. Ratings are calculated automatically from those figures and are not anyone's opinion.
