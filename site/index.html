    How the rating works

    Best right now looks at output alone: goals and assists per game over the last few seasons, weighted towards the most recent, and how many games that's based on.

    Best going forward starts from the same number, then adds a boost for younger players and those on the way up, and lowers it for older players who are unlikely to improve. Use it when you're signing someone for more than one season.

    A rating of 90 against 88 means both are strong, not that one is clearly better. The rating can't see attitude, fitness or style of play. That still needs someone to watch them.

    How leagues compare

    A goal in the Championship is harder to come by than a goal in the Highland League. We work out how much harder by following players who moved between leagues and seeing how their numbers changed. League Two is the baseline.
