What "League Two equivalent" means

A goal in the Highland League is easier to come by than a goal in the Championship. To compare players fairly, each season's goals and assists are multiplied by a rate for the league they were scored in, with Scottish League Two fixed at 1.00. The result is the output we would expect from that player at League Two level.

The rates are learned from players who actually moved between leagues and how their output changed. Leagues with only a few such moves get a rougher rate that is pulled towards 1.00, and seasons in a league with no rate at all are left out of the figure.

Limitations

Goals and assists only

Pressing, hold-up play, movement, aerial work and chance quality are not measured. Penalties count the same as open-play goals, and how assists are recorded varies between leagues. Two players with the same numbers can be very different players.

Games, not minutes

A substitute appearance counts as a game, so a player used mainly off the bench can look less productive per game than he really is. Small numbers of games swing the figures a lot.

Estimates and partial data

League rates are estimates and rougher for leagues with few moves. Contract dates come from Transfermarkt, are listed for only some players, and can be out of date. Wages are not known.
